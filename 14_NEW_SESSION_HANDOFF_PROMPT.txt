Critical rule:
SUNOVATE and SUN Job Monitor are different projects.

- SUN Job Monitor = separate standalone operational pilot.
- SUNOVATE = broader SUN-wide integrated management & knowledge platform.

Do NOT describe SUNOVATE as a rebrand, expansion, or bigger version of SUN Job Monitor.

SUNOVATE major areas:
1. Management Cockpit
2. SUN Business
3. Kinerja Tim Kerja
4. Knowledge Hub
5. Reporting & Analytics
6. Integrations

8. Security & Governance

Important boundaries:
- Kemenkeu Satu remains the official correspondence system.
- SharePoint / document repositories remain source-of-truth for files where appropriate.
- Hermes Agent is the AI orchestration layer, not the database.
- Hermes must inherit user permissions.
- Sensitive AI writes require human approval and audit.
- Do not claim mock integrations are real production integrations.
- Do not invent SUN business logic or data sources that have not been validated.
