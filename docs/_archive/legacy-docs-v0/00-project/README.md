# 00 — Project Baseline

**Status:** CONFIRMED  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-01

## Identity

- **CONFIRMED:** SUNOVATE means **SUN Integrated Management and Knowledge Platform**.
- **CONFIRMED:** owner domain is Direktorat Surat Utang Negara (SUN), DJPPR.
- **CONFIRMED:** internship delivery period is 21 September 2026–20 March 2027.
- **CONFIRMED:** SUNOVATE is distinct from the frozen standalone SUN Job Monitor pilot; it is not a rebrand or expansion of that application.

## Product intent

SUNOVATE is intended to connect management visibility, team work, SUN business information, institutional knowledge, reporting, controlled integrations, and permission-aware AI assistance without replacing authoritative external systems.

## Working principles

1. Label facts as **CONFIRMED**, **PROPOSED**, **OPEN QUESTION**, or **OUT OF SCOPE**.
2. Do not invent SUN figures, business rules, approval chains, data sources, or organizational ranks.
3. Use only records explicitly marked `SIMULATED_DATA` in development and demonstrations.
4. Keep human accountability for sensitive decisions and writes.
5. Treat integrations as unavailable until access and behavior are validated.

## Success boundary

- **PROPOSED:** deliver a demonstrable modular-monolith foundation, validated documentation, representative simulated workflows, and a handover package during the stated period.
- **OUT OF SCOPE:** asserting enterprise production readiness or replacing Kemenkeu Satu, SharePoint, Power BI, BI systems, or other official repositories.
- **OPEN QUESTION:** mentor-approved MVP acceptance criteria and priority module sequence.
