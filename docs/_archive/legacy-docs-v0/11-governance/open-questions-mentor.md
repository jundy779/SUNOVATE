# Open Questions for Mentor

**Status:** NEEDS MENTOR VALIDATION  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-01

These questions block detailed business design. No answer should be inferred.

1. What is the validated function, scope, ownership, and data boundary of **SUN Tematik**?
2. Which user groups and roles may access each module and classification level?
3. What is the real approval flow for knowledge publication, correction, supersession, and archival?
4. Which records require approval, and who is authorized to approve them?
5. Which SUN Business activities are in the internship MVP, and which are read-only?
6. Which system is authoritative for each data domain and document type?
7. What classifications exist, and what are their handling and retention rules?
8. Which source formats and languages must Knowledge Hub ingestion support?
9. Who may use Hermes, for which read actions, and for which proposed write actions?
10. What citation quality and no-answer behavior are acceptable for RAG?
11. Is local development auth acceptable for the demonstration, and what production SSO is approved later?
12. What environments, hosting platform, network restrictions, and deployment approvals apply?
13. What reporting outputs are required, and which remain owned by Power BI or another system?
14. What measurable mentor acceptance criteria define a successful handover on 20 March 2027?
