# 01 — Product

**Status:** NEEDS MENTOR VALIDATION  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-01

## Product modules

- **PROPOSED:** Management Cockpit surfaces attention items without inventing business thresholds.
- **PROPOSED:** SUN Business begins as read-only metadata and contextual views.
- **PROPOSED:** Kinerja Tim Kerja manages outputs, assignments, progress, milestones, pending matters, meetings, follow-ups, and inspection support.
- **PROPOSED:** Knowledge Hub manages searchable, governed SUN knowledge.
- **PROPOSED:** Reporting & Analytics uses validated data only.
- **PROPOSED:** Integrations are provider adapters and mocks until access is granted.
- **PROPOSED:** Hermes Agent performs permission-aware retrieval and proposes, but does not autonomously execute, sensitive writes.
- **PROPOSED:** Security & Governance spans every module.

## Users and teams

The six **CONFIRMED** team labels are PPSUN, AKPSUN, PSUN, PPPSUN, DHTV, and SUN Tematik. They are data scopes, not invented organizational hierarchies. Role names, reporting lines, and cross-team visibility remain **OPEN QUESTION**.

| User need | Initial product response | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Find current work requiring attention | Filtered Cockpit summaries | PROPOSED |
| Track work and follow-up | Kinerja records with audit history | PROPOSED |
| Find authoritative context | Knowledge metadata, citation, and source link | PROPOSED |
| Ask a grounded question | Hermes through authorized APIs | PROPOSED |
| Review activity | Reports and append-only audit events | PROPOSED |

## Information architecture

Primary navigation is proposed as Dashboard, SUN Business, Kinerja Tim Kerja, Knowledge Hub, Reporting & Analytics, Ask SUNOVATE, and Administration. Visibility is permission-aware, but hiding a menu never replaces backend authorization.

## Product boundaries

- **CONFIRMED:** SUNOVATE is SUN Integrated Management and Knowledge Platform.
- **OUT OF SCOPE:** presenting SUNOVATE as SUN Job Monitor or importing that pilot's assumptions without validation.
- **OUT OF SCOPE:** full transaction processing, official correspondence replacement, or autonomous AI action.
- **OPEN QUESTION:** exact MVP priorities, dashboard indicators, report definitions, and SUN Tematik function.
